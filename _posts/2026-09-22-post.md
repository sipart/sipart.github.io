## And Finally
* A lasting physical mark I've left on the world, albeit a small one, is the seating in the British Library in London. In the early 1990s, I worked on the project that produced the chairs, machining the prototypes and production samples from designs by the late, great furniture designer [Ron Carter](https://www.theguardian.com/artanddesign/2013/may/30/ronald-carter-obituary){:target="_blank"}. It's nice to think that many of those chairs are still in daily use more than 30 years later. Here's a photo of the British Library Reading Room with the [aforementioned chairs](https://www.bl.uk/images/v5dwkion/production/f7085b7fe238589734fdbbde5c7d99bdcdc670bd-6608x4405.jpg/humanities-reading-room-british-library-london-2024.jpg?w=1440&auto=format){:target="_blank"}
